team.html

Result: The team page initially produced two heading hierarchy errors during validation. The first error flagged that the heading jumped from <h1> directly to <h3>, skipping <h2>. The second error flagged that headings then jumped from <h2> to <h4>. Both were resolved by restructuring the headings, member names were changed from <h3> to <h2> and the expandable detail subheadings were changed from <h4> to <h3>. After these corrections the page passed with zero errors. This taught me that heading levels must follow a strict sequential order not for visual reasons but for accessibility and standards compliance.
